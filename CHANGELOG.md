## 0.9.1 — Three pages: Answer, Knowledge and The AI

- Page: the per-question menu on the left and the summary bar at the top are gone. The switch at the top has only "Answer" (with a count of the decisions waiting for you), "Knowledge" and "The AI". On first open it starts on Answer when something waits for you, otherwise on Knowledge, and the page you picked opens next time.
- Answer: what waits for a person across the four questions you answer is merged into one queue and shown one card at a time. The question appears at the top of the card and above the buttons, and the button bar sticks to the bottom of the screen (long conversations no longer push the buttons out of view). Bands, model marks, number cards and filters are not shown. Questions only the AI answers, and records whose truth comes from results, are not shown. Only when a new wording waits for adoption does this page ask whether it means the same. The keys (1-9, Enter, →) work only on this page.
- The AI: one line per question with how far it has learned; pressing it opens that question (progress, the last calibration, the lane switch, reviewing and changing past answers, wording candidates). The lessons delivered and the routine, the guards, the decision model and how often it waits for a person are here too. The cluster of model and count pills beside the question title, the three number cards, the project and kind filters and the one-by-one / list switch were removed.
- Section headings sit in a light grey band with dark bold text and a green bar, so they no longer blend into the items below.
- Lesson delivery: a subagent (Claude Code's Agent tool) now gets lessons chosen by the task its parent handed it. A hook just before the Agent / Task tool (`PreToolUse`, `shikii hook agent-task`) keeps the task text and the subagent type, and when the subagent starts (`SubagentStart`) the oldest kept task of the same type is used. Without a kept task it does not choose by the parent's request; it delivers only rules whose scene is "いつも" (or "always"). Before, lessons were chosen by the parent's request and unrelated ones reached the subagent (in this project's records, 4 of 37 deliveries to subagents helped). The update adds a fourth hook to Claude Code (not to Codex; other people's PreToolUse groups are left alone).
- Lesson delivery: history (`history`) articles are no longer delivered automatically, before requests or to subagents (in this project's records they were delivered 7 times and never used). They can still be read by searching (`wiki query`). Articles without a kind (from before the update) are delivered as before.
- Habits: when the agent itself creates or changes a rule, test or procedure (a command, a script), it writes it to the wiki in the same turn even if nobody asked, as a howto or rule page (adding to or editing the closest page). Before, the habits only asked it to save what the person corrected or decided, so procedures it made lived only in the repository and its reply. To reach agents that do not read the skill, the rule is in the skill, the once-per-session habit notice, the reflection request, the shikii section of AGENTS.md and the MCP instructions (the AGENTS.md section is replaced on update).
- Reflection: `reflect submit` (MCP `shikii_reflect`, `/v1/reflect`) no longer limits how many conversations (2), items (4 provisional assessments, 2 wiki drafts, 4 memory assessments) or article reviews (2) it takes at once. The only limit left is the input size, raised from 64 KB to 1 MiB (the same as a work-record write; 64 KB filled up with one to three large drafts). When it is exceeded, the reply says to split the drafts and send the rest in the next submit. Before, the existing sources of a page being extended were counted as conversations, so a page with three or more sources could not be updated through reflection. Saving keeps the existing sources. `reflect query` still shows the oldest two conversations at a time.
- Knowledge: the `rule` kind is renamed from "Rules to keep" to "Rules decided in this project", and a panel with that name sits at the top of the Lessons tab. It lists every rule set by people or by the AI, whatever scene is picked, so rules the AI made are visible to people and none gets buried. "Always" rules come first, then the newest; rules added or changed in the last 7 days are marked "new". The hook and MCP texts and refusal messages use the new name.
- Answer: answering lowers "N left" and the count beside "Answer" at once (before, they changed only at the next 15-second reload). Answering while a reload is in flight no longer brings the answered card back from the old response. The folded section inside Knowledge is renamed "Detailed records of the articles" so it is not confused with the new "The AI" page.
- Cards: Markdown bold and heading marks in the agent's reply are removed for display (no HTML is rendered; also inside the folded previous reply). "Spot-check an AI assessment" now reads "A spot check of a decision the AI made alone", and "The AI could not assess this" reads "The AI could not decide this one".

The request hook lists five at a time (id, title, lesson, when it applies, and the scenes in use) and has the agent set them with `shikii wiki classify <page ID> <kind> <scene>` (`operation: "classify"` on MCP's `shikii_wiki`). The lesson text is not changed, and the result is kept as a new revision. If the article is edited meanwhile, classify refuses (an edit is never undone by the old lesson), and the previous change reason is kept. While the wiki has an unreadable or hand-edited article, no articles are asked for; one line suggests `shikii wiki lint` instead (no block). If the agent tries to end with listed articles still unset, Stop blocks once (the built-in routine check `wiki-kinds`, shown under `rituals:` in `shikii status` and as `kinds` in `/v1/status`). The `wiki query` listing also returns how many remain and the next five.
- Reaching agents without hooks: the reply of `wiki query` starts with the unclassified notice (`unclassified_how`, `unclassified_count`, the next five), and the replies of `wiki ingest`, `wiki classify` and `reflect submit` carry what remains (left out once none remain). The MCP server instructions and the `shikii_wiki` description also describe kind, scene and `classify`.
- The shikii section of AGENTS.md (read by Codex and OpenCode) now describes the kind and scene of lessons, and says to set them within the turn when a reply lists unclassified articles. At the end of an update (`hook install`), each registered project's AGENTS.md that has a shikii section gets the new content (only when exactly one section is enclosed by marker-only lines. Markers inside a person's text are not counted, the text a person wrote around it is left alone, and no section is created in a file without one. A link to CLAUDE.md or similar is written through only when it points inside the repo; a link pointing outside, or one marker or three or more, is left alone and the reason is printed. CRLF files stay CRLF).
- Skill: the wiki guide describes how to choose and set kind and scene. The steps for moving a memory ("move the memory into shikii's wiki") give each lesson a kind and scene, preferring the scenes `place` returns. The hook text that asks for reflection adds `kind` and `scene` to its wiki fields.
- Page: the Knowledge section has "Lessons" and "Work records" tabs. On the Lessons tab, pick a scene on the left (articles without a kind and scene are under "Unclassified"); the middle lists titles by kind, and pressing a title shows on the right the lesson, when it applies, the background, related articles, the number of sources and the last update. The recent list of delivered lessons shows only the start of each text; press it for the whole.

It is not built for a person to check articles one by one. The counts of searches, reviews and reflections, the tree of earlier articles, and earlier claims with their check buttons moved into the folded "The AI in detail". The inbox groups items into one per live article, with a count. `/v1/wiki` returns `lesson_meta` (each article's last update and its helpful and harmful counts), `inbox` and `unclassified_count`.

A draft with word pairs not in the merged page's current lesson (it added content) is now compared on those pairs only, and a restating draft is compared on the whole and ranked lower (a merge that only placed a restating draft is still labeled).

## 0.8.3 — Tell the person when a line is drawn, or why it cannot be

- Wiki placement truths: a draft merged into an existing page usually has its lesson rewritten (and is often saved with `wiki ingest`), so the placement judgment got no truth; only "made a new page" outcomes were labeled, every truth was "no", and no line could ever be drawn. Now `reflect submit` and `wiki ingest` (CLI, MCP, HTTP) both record the outcome. A saved draft matches a placed one when its lesson is the same; otherwise only a merge or replacement matches, and only a draft placed within 24 hours that had that page among its candidates and whose placed lesson mostly (60% of its word pairs; space-separated scripts such as English are compared by words, Japanese by characters) appears in the saved title, lesson and body. The best-covered draft wins (the newer one on a tie), so when two drafts are placed at once, the merged one is usually labeled. When a merge keeps the page's lesson and only adds to the body, the page's own old placement record is not taken as an exact match. Placed records whose candidate pages are gone are ignored. A new page never matches loosely, so unrelated saves do not label abandoned drafts.
- Line states: once the truths reach `eval.min_n`, `shikii status` and the page no longer just say "ready to calibrate". They count truths the way automatic calibration does (the latest per input, from people and results only) and tell apart "only one answer so far" (yes/no questions only), "tried: the answers could not be separated (tried again as truths grow)" (only for the model that was tried) and "enough truths: the line is drawn automatically". `/v1/status` returns `one_answer` and `not_separable` as key states.
- The Stop hook tells the person once (a line in the terminal in Claude Code) when a line is newly drawn and when there are enough truths but no line can be drawn, with the reason. The wording depends on the question: for the question a person answers (needs-plan), the rest still asks; for the hook questions settled by results, the rest waits for results; for the wiki questions nothing is asked and lessons are never replaced silently. When answers cannot be separated, the hook questions ask the agent for other wordings; the others are tried again as truths grow. Once per question; again only when the state, the wording or the model changes.

- `shikii-readout` passes through only upstreams that reject the probe with 400/422; 401/403/405/429 fail startup with the reason.
- `shikii status` prints the delivered-lessons and guards lines even without records, no longer counts the guard records (`records/guards.jsonl`) as a decision, and reads the store's `decisions/*.yaml` when no spec path is given, so its numbers match the page.
- Page: keyboard shortcuts (1–9 / Enter / →) act only on the unanswered decisions; the Japanese learning line reads "n / m"; non-JSON responses (a reverse proxy's HTML) are reported by status code; summary times use the chosen language.
- Skill: `import-memory.md` — one instruction ("move the memory into shikii's wiki") makes the agent keep each memory file as a captured source, draft one lesson per rule, merge through `place`, and turn the memory folder into a thin index pointing at the wiki pages. Installed with the skill by `--init`.
- Documentation: `shikii new same-issue --from` before the README loop, Go is required by the installer, the page starts at the first pending answer, the files `--init` adds, `--specs examples` in the guide's quick start, hook counts, the number of built-in questions, the silent wipe output when updating from 0.6.0, and the skill's place step, CLI JSON shapes and the variants JSON.

## 0.7.3 — Corrections become guards, lessons reach subagents

- **Turn a person's corrections into guards that stop the turn.** They live in each project's `.shikii/guards/<id>.yaml` (created by `--init`). Three kinds and no other options: `paired-files` (when one file changed, its partner must have changed too, e.g. CHANGELOG.md and CHANGELOG_ja.md), `forbidden-words` (added lines must not contain the listed words; excepted paths are not checked), `commit-body` (every commit made in the turn must contain the required words in its body). At the end of the turn (Stop) the enabled guards are applied to the changed files, the added lines and the commits since the HEAD recorded at the request; a failing guard returns Claude Code's `decision: block` with the reason so the agent fixes the work. The continuation after a stop is never blocked again; whether it was fixed is recorded in `records/guards.jsonl`. When the listing or the HEAD is unavailable the guard passes (stop only when certain). `shikii guard list | add | enable | disable | try`: `add` writes a disabled draft from JSON, `enable` first shows the kind's negative example stopping (RED) and the positive example passing (GREEN) and refuses otherwise. The reflection notice and the skill tell the agent to draft a guard from a mechanically checkable correction and to ask the person before enabling. In the 2026-10-23 experiments, lessons the agent could already read in the repository changed nothing (4 of 5 tasks tied), delivered lessons it could not know otherwise were followed 6 of 6 times, and guards fixed 6 of 6 turns (details in the guide).
- **Deliver lessons to subagents.** `hook install` registers Claude Code's `SubagentStart` hook (not for Codex, which has no such event); when a subagent starts, lessons close to the parent's request are delivered in the same form. The parent's session state is untouched and the delivery is recorded under its own ID so reflection can mark it helpful or unused. Until 0.7.2 subagents received nothing.
- **Count in production whether delivered lessons changed anything.** `shikii status` now prints the delivered quotes, the helpful / unused / harmful / unknown assessments and the ones still awaiting assessment, the last 7 days, and correction deliveries (followed / repeated). ## 0.3.0 — Grow a wiki from everyday conversations

The regular agent turns requests, AI replies and subsequent human messages into lessons with applicability conditions. shikii manages sources, the index, earlier revisions and history, then quotes lessons for similar requests. No wiki-specific UI work or additional resident model is required.

- **Ingest:** hooks provide up to two unprocessed conversations for the agent to organize through MCP `shikii_wiki` or `shikii wiki ingest`. Conversations without a reusable lesson, such as an additional request, can be archived as processed while retaining their source text.
- **Search:** `query` returns lessons, unprocessed conversations and original sources from the same project. Short search terms can find long lessons. The knowledge view also shows lessons and sources.
- **Update:** updating a lesson preserves its sources and previous revisions. Replacing an old lesson makes references use the new one. Replacement can be undone, and reverting content still leaves a history entry.
- **Check:** `lint` verifies source hashes, generated pages, revisions and links, and repairs the generated index. Runs once a day at the end of a turn as well.
- **Distinguish certainty:** all lessons are hypotheses with sources. Excludes lessons whose sources are missing or changed, and does not count AI interpretations as human answers or calibration samples.
- **Check versions:** aligns `shikii version`, MCP and the plugin at `0.3.0`.

The workflow of retaining sources, having AI edit a wiki, and running ingestion, search and periodic checks draws on [Andrej Karpathy's LLM Wiki idea](https://gist.github.com/karpathy/442a6bf555914893e9891c11519de94f). Combined with shikii's claims, evidence and retesting, it distinguishes conversational interpretation from verified results.

Existing users can update the executables and agent instructions to use this through the existing two hooks. New MCP tools appear when the server next starts. Until then, agents can use the CLI commands supplied by the hooks.
